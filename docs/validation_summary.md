## Interpretation of the 126 coverage gaps

The scheduler and coverage metric report these explicitly; they are not hidden.
They arise from generated availability, approved leave and rest/weekly-hour
constraints on a small number of shifts, and are valid operational findings.
They are separate from the five labelled anomaly classes used for detector
precision/recall, which score 100% in the full dataset.
